Sign in to Sidedoor's browser

  1. Make sure Google Chrome is installed. Click Open Sidedoor's browser. A Chrome window opens. It is separate from your normal Chrome: your usual tabs and logins are not there.
  2. Go to linkedin.com in that window and sign in. LinkedIn may ask for a code or flag a new device; approve it.
  3. Go to your email website (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo or other) and sign in with your application email, the one in your profile. Only that account: Sidedoor reads application receipts there and checks it is the same address. Pick that website under "Your webmail" (choose Other and paste its address if it is not listed).
  4. Click I'm done. Sidedoor closes its browser for you.
  5. Run the email check (step 9).

If Sidedoor says its browser "did not shut down cleanly": quit Google Chrome completely (Chrome menu > Quit Google Chrome), click Try again, and restart your computer if it still says so.
