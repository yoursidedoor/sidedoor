Sign in to Sidedoor's browser

  1. Under "Your webmail", pick your mailbox's website.
  2. Click Open Sidedoor's browser.
  3. In that window, sign in to the mailbox of your application email.
  4. Click I'm done.
If something goes wrong

If Sidedoor says a different mailbox is signed in, click Open Sidedoor's browser, sign in to your application email, then click I'm done again.

If Sidedoor says its browser "did not shut down cleanly", {% if guide_platform == "windows" %}close every Chrome window{% else %}quit Google Chrome completely (Chrome menu > Quit Google Chrome){% endif %} and click Open Sidedoor's browser again. Restart your computer if it still says so.
