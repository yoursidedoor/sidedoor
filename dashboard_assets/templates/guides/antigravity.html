Antigravity with your Google account

Google's terms don't allow apps like Sidedoor to run Antigravity. If you choose it, Google may suspend your Antigravity or Gemini CLI account.

  1. Install the Antigravity command-line app (agy). On a Mac with Homebrew, open Terminal and paste: 
    brew install --cask antigravity-cli
    Press Return. Otherwise follow Google's install steps for the Antigravity CLI.
  2. In Terminal type agy and press Return. A browser window opens: sign in with your Google account. When agy shows its prompt, close Terminal.
  3. Back in Sidedoor, choose Antigravity and click Check. Sidedoor adds permission rules for its own tools to Antigravity's settings, so its runs can use them; every other tool stays refused.

What is Terminal? On a Mac: Applications > Utilities > Terminal.
