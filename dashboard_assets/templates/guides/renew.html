Renew your license

  1. Click Renew.
  2. Finish the checkout.
  3. Copy the new license key on the page you land on. It starts with SD-.
  4. In Settings > License, paste it in the "Bought a new key? Paste it here" box.
  5. Click Change key.
If something goes wrong

If the page says your key isn't ready yet, wait a minute and reload it.

The page shows your key for 24 hours. After that, use its "Email me my key" link, or contact support@yoursidedoor.com.
