Buy and enter your license

  1. Go to yoursidedoor.com and click Start 14-day free trial.
  2. Pay through Stripe's checkout (a card is needed to start the 14-day free trial; $44.99 a month after, tax not included).
  3. You land on a page showing your license key, starting with SD-. Copy it now. Reloading the page shows it again, but only for 24 hours after you paid.
  4. Paste it into step 1 and click Activate.
  5. If the page shows only {"error": "CLAIM_NOT_FOUND"}: right after paying, the payment is still being recorded, so wait a minute and reload. More than 24 hours after paying, the page has expired: contact support@yoursidedoor.com.
  6. Later, to update your card, see invoices or cancel: Settings > License > Manage subscription, then sign in with the email you subscribed with.
