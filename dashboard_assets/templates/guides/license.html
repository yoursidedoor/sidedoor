Start your free trial

  1. On yoursidedoor.com, click Start 14-day free trial.
  2. Finish the checkout.
  3. Copy the license key on the page you land on. It starts with SD-.
  4. Paste it in the License key box.
  5. Click Activate.
If something goes wrong

If the page says your key isn't ready yet, wait a minute and reload it.

The page shows your key for 24 hours. After that, use its "Email me my key" link, or contact support@yoursidedoor.com.
