Connect (or reconnect) LinkedIn

  1. Click Connect LinkedIn, then Open the LinkedIn sign-in page. A Unipile page opens in a new tab; Unipile is the service Sidedoor uses to send LinkedIn messages for you.
  2. Sign in with your LinkedIn email and password on that page.
  3. If LinkedIn asks for a code (email, text or authenticator app) or to confirm on your phone, do it there. This is normal the first time.
  4. When the page says you are connected, close the tab and come back. Sidedoor notices within a few seconds.
  5. The link works for one hour. If it expires, click Get a new link.

Reconnect: the same steps, starting with Reconnect LinkedIn. LinkedIn signs connected tools out from time to time.
