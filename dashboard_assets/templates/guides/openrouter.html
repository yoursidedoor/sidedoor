OpenRouter: account, credits, API key

  1. Go to openrouter.ai and click Sign in or Sign up; create an account.
  2. Open the Credits page and add credits. OpenRouter accepts major credit cards, AliPay and USDC crypto. Tip: turn on auto top-up so your balance refills below a threshold you choose.
  3. How much to add: start with a small amount; Sidedoor shows what it has used.
  4. Open the Keys page and create a key: give it a name (for example "Sidedoor") and choose a credit limit or no limit (daily, weekly or monthly limits are possible; a request over the limit is refused until it resets).
  5. Copy the key right away: OpenRouter shows it only once. Paste it into step 2. Click Save and test.
  6. If the test says "no credits", go back to step 2 of this guide.
